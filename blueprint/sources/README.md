## Scope and provenance

The roadmap covers all fifteen chapters, all embedded exercises, and the
separate exercise collection. The PDFs are copyrighted and are not published
with this site. Retrieval metadata, source reconciliation, statement
ambiguities, exercise ownership, and the reviewed Phase 1 graph are tracked in
the repository's `sources/` directory.

Node summaries are formalization specifications and navigation aids; they do
not replace inspection of the adopted source revision.
