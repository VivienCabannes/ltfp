# Open-measurable and Borel spaces

Let a type `X` carry both a topology and a measurable-space structure. Its Borel measurable space is the sigma-algebra generated by the open subsets.

`OpensMeasurableSpace X` means that the Borel sigma-algebra is contained in the given measurable space. Equivalently, every open set is measurable; complements then show that every closed set is measurable as well. `BorelSpace X` is the stronger compatibility condition that the given measurable space equals the Borel sigma-algebra.

These are compatibility properties of topology and measurability. They introduce no measure, finiteness, separation, or countability assumption.
